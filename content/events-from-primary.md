## Notes (mandatory caveats)

1. **Agenda label ≠ proven chip owner.** “Alexandre de Moraes BRASILIA” (+ aliases) is the label in Vorcaro’s address book for terminal +55 61 9266-4093. PF attributes the terminal to that contact; **Moraes’s handset was not forensically examined** in IPJ-A 3298613/2026. Do not equate the label with proven personal use of the SIM.
2. **View-once / 24h disappearing messages incomplete.** From 2025-09-17 the chat uses 24h ephemeral messages; on 2025-11-17 PF logs **5 view-once** sends; on 2025-10-01 some messages show as deleted by sender. **Most replies from that contact were not recovered.** Vorcaro often wrote in Apple Notes → screenshot → WhatsApp.
3. **Barci disputes 2nd contract / dação.** Escritório Barci told Folha (via Poder360) it signed **only** the Jan/2024 Master contract; denied transfer/substitution; said the May/2025 Viking quitação proposal was **not accepted** (“nada foi assinado”) and the Master contract ended with liquidation. Viking contract + dação PDFs exist in the published set — dispute is over acceptance/validity, not file existence.
4. **PF analysis is non-exhaustive** (72h). Names “Andrei”/“Paulo”/“G”/“Galípolo” are PF or press readings unless quoted. Prefer **20:48 UTC-3** for “estou online”. Allegations remain allegations unless a court judgment is cited.
